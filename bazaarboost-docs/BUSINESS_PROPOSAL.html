3. Vulnerability Audit & Mitigation Roadmap

⚠️

Vulnerability: Offline Manual Receipts

Risk: Shoppers submitting altered or fake bank transfer receipt screenshots.

Mitigation: Dual Tesseract.js OCR reading transaction ID + mandatory duplication check across database reference IDs before approving.

📦

Vulnerability: Multi-Vendor Shipping

Risk: High shipping costs when shoppers buy items from 3 vendors in one cart.

Mitigation: Order splitting into Sub-Orders. Each vendor dispatches directly via courier partners (PostEx/Trax) with zero platform warehouse overhead.

🌐

Vulnerability: Server Socket Scaling

Risk: High concurrent WebSocket connections stalling single Node.js loop.

Mitigation: Redis Adapter integration across Node.js instances to support 50,000+ active negotiation sockets concurrently.

4. Platform Revenue Mathematics & Unit Economics

Sales Commission Breakdown Formula

Gross Order Total = (Subtotal - Coupon Discount) + Shipping Fee
Platform Commission = (Subtotal - Coupon Discount) × Take Rate %
Vendor Payout = Gross Total - Platform Commission - Shipping Fee

Worked Numerical Case Study (Rs. 10,000 Order)

  • Itemized Subtotal: Rs. 10,000
  • Promo Coupon Discount (10%): -Rs. 1,000 (Net Subtotal: Rs. 9,000)
  • Same-City Rider Shipping Premium: +Rs. 150
  • Shopper Total Collected: Rs. 9,150
  • Platform Revenue (5% Base Commission): Rs. 450
  • Vendor Net Payout: Rs. 8,550
